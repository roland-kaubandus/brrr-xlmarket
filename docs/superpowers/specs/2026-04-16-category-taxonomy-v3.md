## Problem

v2 (24 L1) had critical duplicates: generators + solar in two L1s, pressure washers in three places, laser/engraving split across Welding and Laser/CNC. The separate 20-sector proposal fixed some issues but introduced new ones (Pet + Camping forced merge, Salon hidden under Office).

## Solution

**22 L1 categories.** Zero duplicates. B2B buyer persona-driven. Aligned with market research priorities.

## Design Decisions

- B2B-first, accessible to prosumers/DIY
- English category names (store is bilingual ET/EN)
- Market research alignment: HoReCa (#1), Laser/CNC (#2), Printing (#3) as prominent L1s
- Zero duplicates — enforced by duplicate prevention table
- 22 L1 is practical: fits 8×3 grid on desktop with 2 overflow, or 6×4
- Cross-reference system replaces duplicates
