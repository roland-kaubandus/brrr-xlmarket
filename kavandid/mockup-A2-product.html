The VEVOR Metal Lathe 7"x14" represents a significant leap forward for home machinists, small workshops, and educational settings. Powered by a robust 550W brushless motor, this compact yet capable machine delivers the torque and speed range needed to turn steel, aluminum, brass, and plastics with consistent precision.

With a variable speed range spanning 50 to 2,500 RPM and a digital readout for precise speed monitoring, operators can dial in the exact cutting conditions required for any material. The all-metal gear train eliminates the flex and wear associated with plastic gearing, ensuring that the machine maintains its accuracy over thousands of hours of service. The MT3 headstock spindle and MT2 tailstock taper provide compatibility with a wide range of standard tooling and accessories.

The precision-ground cast iron bed provides the rigid foundation essential for accurate turning operations. Cross-slide and compound slide controls feature graduated dials for precise positioning, while the robust tailstock provides steady support for longer workpieces. Whether you are threading bolts, turning shafts, boring cylinders, or facing surfaces, this lathe delivers workshop-grade results in a footprint that fits comfortably on a standard workbench.

Every unit ships with a comprehensive accessory kit including a 3-jaw chuck, tool post set, live center, dead center, and detailed operating manual. The machine arrives partially assembled with clear instructions, allowing most operators to be cutting metal within an hour of unboxing. Built to CE safety standards with integrated emergency stop and spindle guard.
