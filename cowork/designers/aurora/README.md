# AURORA — Editorial / Swiss / International

Six mockups for XLMarket's homepage category module and category view, designed in an editorial-Pentagram-Swiss register: typography as the primary tool, IBM Plex Serif paired with DM Sans, off-white paper (`#fafaf7` / `#f4f4f0`), deep ink (`#0a0a0a`), and amber (`#D97706`) as the single accent. Hairline dividers, tabular numerals, small-caps labels, no shadows, no boxed logo.

## Homepage variants

**homepage-v1 — Editorial grid with feature-of-the-week.**
A newspaper-style masthead sets the issue number and a serif headline, then the 22 disciplines sit in a 12-column grid of hairline-separated cells. One 6×2 cell breaks the rhythm as a photographed "Feature of the week" (HoReCa), and a paired HVAC + pull-quote strip creates a second editorial grid-break. Below the index, a 4-column Starter-kits band carries the B2B proposition. Concept: read the catalogue like a magazine index.

**homepage-v2 — The Index: vertical list with large numerals.**
Every discipline becomes a horizontal line with a 300-size IBM Plex Serif numeral on the left, a serif category name in the middle, product counts and sub-cat counts on the right. Three lines carry a small atmosphere thumbnail as a punctuation break. Hovering reveals a one-line description and slides the row 14 px right with a warm amber wash. A black "Trade Desk" block closes the page. Concept: one long table of contents — scannable, disciplined, quiet.

**homepage-v3 — Asymmetric bento with four hero categories.**
The top half of the index is a mixed-span grid: four dark image-hero tiles (HoReCa 6×2, Laser 3×2, Welding 3×2, Printing 6×2) lead the visual rhythm; the remaining eighteen disciplines follow as text-only tiles of 3/4/6 spans with occasional paper-2 accent fills. A two-column starter-kit teaser closes with one dark "configurator" side and one paper "featured kit" side. Concept: editorial bento — the four disciplines that matter most photographed, the eighteen supporting acts typeset.

## Category-view variants (HoReCa & Food Service)

**category-v1 — Editorial table + products grid with sticky filter rail.**
Atmosphere strip at the top acts as a cinema letterbox; a two-line masthead summarises 612 products / 6 sub-cats / 24% VAT / kit-CTA. The six sub-categories form a proper editorial table with index numerals, sub-cat name, a descriptive column and right-aligned counts. Below, a 260 px sticky filter rail sits beside a 3-column product grid of 12 cards. Concept: sub-cats are a table of contents, products are the body text.

**category-v2 — Split view with left sticky rail.**
A 300-px sticky column on the left combines the six sub-cats (as filled rows with active state) with the full filter stack and a dark "Trade desk" contact block. The right column is a dense 4-across product grid (16 SKUs) with active-filter chips, sort and per-page controls above. Small atmosphere strip lives inline with the head, not above it. Concept: the rail is the entire navigation of the discipline — no scrolling away from it.

**category-v3 — Magazine feed: chips + long-caption editorial cards.**
A full magazine masthead with a drop-cap dek and a three-column stats strip opens the page. Sub-categories become a sticky chip bar; filters become dropdown buttons underneath. Products are presented as editorial articles with long captions, specs strips, and genuine prose — one 12-span "Editor's pick" opens the feed, the rest mix 6-span and 4-span cards. Concept: the category page reads like a section opener in a trade monthly — each product earns a real caption.
