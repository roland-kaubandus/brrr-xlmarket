# NERO — Bento / Neo-Brutalist / Bold Geometric

Industrial B2B weight with Apple-bento asymmetry and Vercel-OSS boldness. Thick 2.5–3px borders, hard offset shadows that click into place on hover, DM Sans Black at oversized sizes, JetBrains Mono for tabular product counts. Color system: `#0c1119` ink, `#fcfcfc` paper, `#f59e0b` amber and `#f97316` safety-orange as the working accents, with `#06b6d4` electric cyan reserved for "new" badges. All six mockups share the same token system so they feel like one product.

---

## Homepage variants

**`homepage-v1.html` — True Bento.** Twenty-two categories in a 12-column asymmetric grid with cells that span 2/3/4 columns and selectively double in height. Four categories are feature tiles with atmosphere photos bleeding to the edges; the rest are solid-color tiles (amber, orange, cyan, lime, ink) with chunky filled SVG icons in the bottom-right corner. A lime Starter-Kit tile and a dark Newsletter tile break the grid rhythm so the page feels like a curated display wall rather than a directory. Serves entrepreneurs by making 22 very different trades equally browsable and letting visual weight carry hierarchy.

**`homepage-v2.html` — Mega Drawer + Starter Kits.** A strong `All 22 Categories` button sits at the top next to search, and a visible mega drawer on dark ink lists every L1 L-shape below. The main 6×4 bento has two `kit-tile` blocks (`Café Complete €2,799` and `Auto-Shop Base €4,540`) that physically break the grid and show itemised contents with prices — they are the B2B pitch hiding inside the category module. A services strip below closes with Net-30, volume pricing, dedicated manager, and 24h quote. This variant is the most sales-forward of the three.

**`homepage-v3.html` — Inverted Dark Bento.** Navy-on-ink base with white borders and amber/paper shadows. Atmosphere photos run through a navy-tint filter that lifts on hover; stark white typographic tiles provide high-contrast counterpoints. A large amber `B2B STARTER KITS` tile anchors the middle of the grid with the full kit ladder from €2,799 to €6,340. This direction is the "dark luxury" read — confident, reduced, the site version of a black-hardbound trade catalogue.

## Category variants (Laser, CNC & Digital Fabrication)

**`category-v1.html` — Bento Sub-Cats + Horizontal Filters.** A compact dark atmosphere strip replaces the giant hero (number + title + 3 stat cells, atmosphere photo fading in from the right). The six subcategories sit in a 6-col bento where `CO2 Laser Engravers & Cutters` is a 3×2 amber feature tile with inline product icon previews. A horizontal filter pill bar with an active `In stock` pill sits above a clean 4-column product grid, each product framed with a 2.5px border and a split `Add to cart` + quick-view action bar. The amber B2B CTA band at the bottom closes with a volume-quote pitch. Best serves entrepreneurs who know the subcategory but need the filter density of a real search result page.

**`category-v2.html` — Left Rail Numbered Blocks.** The six subs become large numbered rail blocks on the left (`02 · Diode Laser Engravers`, active state inverted to ink + amber) with an attached filter panel underneath — price range, brand, stock, rating. The right side is a bento product grid where two `prod--feat` tiles take 2×2 cells and show full specs, bundled-software notes, and B2B-warranty language. An amber B2B band sits directly under the atmosphere strip with the call to "Buy 3+ machines". Best serves workshop buyers who want to compare multiple L3 subs without losing filter context.

**`category-v3.html` — Split Layout (Image Sub-Bento + Tight Product Grid).** Cleanly divides the page: top half is six atmosphere-photo sub tiles in a 3+3 bento, each with amber-bordered numeric labels (`01 · CO2 LASER`, etc.) and navy-tinted images. Bottom half is a white-background tight 4-column product grid with a horizontal pill filter row, ending in a dark navy `b2b-floating` CTA block with an amber-accent clip-path corner. This is the most editorial read — sub-categories feel like chapter cards, products feel like the inventory index underneath. Best serves first-time visitors landing from search who need to orient visually before drilling into products.
