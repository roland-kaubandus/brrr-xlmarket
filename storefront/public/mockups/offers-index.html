Homepage · offers band · 12 concepts

Neli meeskonda (Copy · Product · Design · Taste), igaüks kolm kontseptsiooni puhtalt lehelt. Neist saab valida ühe tervikliku, kombineerida mitut, või äratõukuda.

Team 1 · Copywriter + brand voice
T1-A

Trade Counter

Kolm kolonni, kolm ostja-tüüpi: "I know what I need" (otsing + SKU-counts), "I'm starting something" (päris kit-hinnad), "I buy for a company" (Net-30, Pay-in-3, VAT invoice). No photos, all structure.

→ T1-B

Stock Board

8 päris SKU rida erinevatest kategooriatest. Live stock + lead time. Iga rida klikitav. Tõestab 17,000 SKU depth ilma seda väitmata.

→ T1-C

Procurement Question

Üks lai editorial statement: "The same VEVOR gear you'd import from Shenzhen — landed in Estonia." 3 proof-line, üks CTA. Nimetab päris-konkurendi ja lööb selle kolme numbriga.

→
Team 2 · Product lens
T2-A

Price Receipt

1 päris SKU vs VEVOR.eu direct vs EU retail. Kolm hinda, delta amber. Tagline "Half the Price" lõpuks tõestatud, mitte vaid väidetud. Andmed CMS tabelis, nädalas uuendatud.

→ T2-B

What Broke This Week

Returning-operator'ile. Otsingu-väli "What broke? Part, model, description…" + 4 ships-today SKU + failure-chips (coffee spares, generator kits, welder consumables). Pit-crew positioning.

→ T2-C

Category Grid (18 tiles)

Kustuta riba, too 18 L1-kategooria-tile-grid asemele. Top 3 tume, ülejäänud valge. Teenib Google-first-timer'it. Kõik andmed juba olemas, 1-3 päeva ehitust.

→
Team 3 · Frontend design
T3-A

Inventory Wall

Bloomberg-terminal energia. 220pt tabular numeral "17,043" vasakul + 3 narrow scrolling-column SKU ticker paremal, erinevad scroll-kiirused (12s/18s/26s). Hover pause. Paberi-cream taust.

→ T3-B

One Hand, One Tool

Üks cinematic still-pilt 21:9 edge-to-edge + Fraunces serif headline + amber 160px rule + üks chevron-link. 4 stseeni rotatsioon 4.5s dwell'iga. Aesop/Apple restraint.

→ T3-C

Ledger Stripe

120px tall riba, horisontaalne marquee: 96pt display words + 13pt mono metadata vahelduvad. Amber hairline keskel läbi, katkestub display sõnadest. NYT "Daily" lockup meets Dieter Rams.

→
Team 4 · Design taste
T4-A

Running Sheet

Trade bulletin esileht. Fraunces serif masthead "This week at XLmarket / Week 17, 2026" + 7 dated entry'it (Mon · Apr 24 · new arrival / price cut / low stock / warranty note). Lloyd's List + Monocle Briefing.

→ T4-B

Single Window

Üks edge-to-edge 21:9 pilt + pasted-on gallery-label caption nurgas. Aperture monograph + Patek Philippe "Generations" DNA. Density dial 2. Zero CTAs in frame, üks inline link plaque'i sees.

→ T4-C

Index Strip

Atlas gazetteer-stiilis. 4 orthogonal ways in: By trade (Barista/Baker/Welder…), By room (Kitchen/Workshop/Salon…), By need (Something broke/Opening up/Scaling up), This week. Fraunces body + dot leaders + mono counts.

→
